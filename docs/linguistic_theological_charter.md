# Core Theological & Linguistic Charter: Biblical Language Translation Engine
**Status:** ACTIVATED (Step 2)

## 1. Translation Philosophy: The Integrated Approach
This engine adopts a "Transparent Equivalence" model.
- **Primary Goal:** To provide a translation that preserves the original linguistic structure (formal equivalence) while utilizing the semantic knowledge graph to clarify ancient cultural metaphors and idioms (dynamic/functional equivalence).
- **Theological Baseline:** The engine shall maintain neutrality regarding denominational dogma while strictly adhering to the established, historically recognized critical texts (e.g., Nestle-Aland for Greek, BHS for Hebrew).

## 2. Linguistic Standards
- **Hebrew/Aramaic:** Preservation of the masoretic consonantal text is mandatory. Vowel points and cantillation marks are to be treated as secondary layers, modifiable by user preference.
- **Koine Greek:** The engine must prioritize the koine dialect nuances, maintaining consistency in verbal aspect (e.g., Aorist vs. Present tense distinctions) which are often lost in modern translations.
- **Morphological Parsing:** All parsings must be linked to standard lexicons (e.g., BDAG, HALOT) to ensure scholarly rigor.

## 3. Theological Guardrails
- **Systemic Negation:** Any translation output that negates a core concept of the source text due to algorithmic hallucination will be automatically flagged for manual review by the Theological Audit Layer.
- **Anthropomorphic Sensitivity:** Idioms involving divine anthropomorphism (e.g., "the arm of the Lord") must be translated with contextual notes explaining the literary device to the end-user.
- **Intertextuality:** The system must identify and highlight major NT allusions to OT texts to maintain the integrity of progressive revelation.

## 4. Governance & Revision
- **Expert Oversight:** Final linguistic outputs are subject to validation by the designated scholarly advisory board.
- **Immutable History:** Every change to the translation algorithm or the semantic knowledge graph must be logged with a timestamp and a justification link to the relevant section of this Charter.
